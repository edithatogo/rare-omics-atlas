HM450 technical pair. Preserve the complete probe universe, named sample,
explicit channel/address semantics, missingness and quality-mask tracks. Check

reference receipt. Full QCDPB qualification, genome/gene mapping beyond the
selected coordinate annotation, scientific M-scale error budgets, disease

## Acceptance

1. Strict baseline and installed native hook precede tracked implementation.
2. Required source/assets and complete ordered identities are checked before
   and after actual execution; unavailable or corrupt inputs fail.
3. Every raw/Q production export value and NA is checked against the independently
   generated reference under the declared serialization rule. The same pair at
   one and two threads is a repeatability check, not a multi-sample benchmark.
4. Annotation row-space, channels, controls and unmapped coordinates remain
   explicit; no intersection-only comparison or silent probe deletion is allowed.
5. Meaningful rejection tests, full strict verification, independent review and
   source-bound aggregate evidence pass before bounded archiving.
6. Final integration requires the actual native hook and hosted checks. Keep
   source-level failures, method limits and unobserved device gates visible.

Parallel T01 cohort-source curation owns its own aggregate receipt and does not
admit a cohort or change this technical fixture.
