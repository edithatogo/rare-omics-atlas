## Question and proposed estimand

P1 combines E0, E2 and E3: which KMT2D-associated blood methylation effects and biological interpretations transfer across independently admitted studies, with useful uncertainty and failure boundaries? Compare against the closest original CpG/region/pathway analyses using the [dated contribution review](../../../research/experiment-publication-plan.md#24-november-2026--analytical-gap-and-contribution-review-pre-n1). Generic classification, variant reclassification, extra dataset counts and pipeline execution are not the proposed contribution.

The proposed E2 estimand is the within-study case-minus-native-control coefficient on `M=log2(beta/(1-beta))`, conditional on the declared covariates. Report its scaled ordinary/moderated uncertainty and confidence interval; an M-value coefficient is not a percentage methylation difference or an identified causal effect. Any accompanying beta-scale effect needs its own explicit definition. Proposed E3 outcomes are frozen-feature/region effect direction and magnitude, uncertainty, coverage and pathway transfer. Their exact aggregation, primary endpoint, comparison thresholds and multiplicity must be settled before freeze; no significant-result requirement defines successful execution.
