# T03 — specification

## Objective

Test a focused, falsifiable difference or convergence among Kabuki, Sotos, Weaver, reciprocal dosage and qualified DNMT3A disorders. Generic pathway overlap or adding DNMT3A does not establish novelty.

## Scientific / governance boundary

No execution, downloads or code generation is authorised by this planning track. Any source-dependent assertion requires independently verifiable evidence. Follow `conductor/workflow.md` and Ponytail decisions.

## Inputs and dependencies

See [candidate dataset register](../../../research/datasets.md), [analysis matrix](../../../research/analyses.md), and [journal routing](../../../research/journals.md). This track's relevant studies and exact eligibility must be qualified before use.

## Publication target

This is **E4**, a conditional extension to the default P1 study or part of a later combined P2 transfer/multiomics study. Follow the [experiment/publication plan](../../../research/experiment-publication-plan.md); there is no automatic paper for T03. Combine a small comparison when it supports the same central claim, or retain it as exploratory supporting work.

For a clinically substantive focused comparison, European Journal of Medical Genetics is a candidate, with AJMG Part A as backup. The actual P1/P2 bundle and evidence determine the target under the [journal strategy](../../../research/journals.md).

## Exit criteria

- Distinct conclusions or justified independent replication beyond the published cross-syndrome comparisons, assessed against the dated prior-art review.
- Control matching, shared reused samples, assay platform and tissue factors documented.
- Compare within-study effects, preserve covariance from shared controls, and account for unequal probe/region coverage; do not pool incompatible case-control batches.
- Treat GSE191276's three Sotos cases and three individual controls as corroborative; its pooled parental array is one assay. Admit DNMT3A only with an adequate qualified contrast, not from the two new GSE167230 TBRS records alone.
- Either falsifiable cross-disease hypotheses or a reason to stop at descriptive analysis.

## Stop conditions

Reassess overlapping publications through N0/N2; continue only with a documented distinct contribution or justified replication. Stop if no worthwhile contribution remains, source rights are unclear, validation samples are not independent, the proposed inference is not identifiable, or simpler methods make the extra complexity unnecessary. Record an honest hold rather than invent evidence.
