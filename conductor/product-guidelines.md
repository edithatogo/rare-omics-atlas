# Product and research guidelines

This is a research project, not a diagnostic service. Lead with a clinical question, an identifiable comparison and a modest useful result. Keep discovery, independent replication, software qualification and causal perturbation evidence distinct.

Use exact accessions, original study provenance and stable assay identifiers. Preserve participant ambiguity and source restrictions. Describe counts as assays, specimens or people only when the source supports that unit. Do not publish detailed participant metadata simply because it is discoverable in a source repository.

Prefer one coherent clinical paper with supporting qualification. A catalogue, port or platform comparison can remain infrastructure. Recheck prior art before and after each analysis and immediately before submission.

Use the smallest qualified existing method. Rust is preferred, followed by Julia, Python and R among suitable implementations. The numerical oracle can remain in another language. File contracts permit replacement without changing the scientific question.

Maintain one authoritative context, one owner per edited path and explicit evidence for completed tasks. Follow the copied [general guide](code_styleguides/general.md) and central [scientific-native](https://github.com/edithatogo/repository-standards/blob/main/docs/scientific-native.md) and [Rust dependency](https://github.com/edithatogo/repository-standards/blob/main/docs/rust-dependency-selection-standard.md) standards. The pinned Conductor distribution has no Rust-specific style asset; do not invent an upstream one.
