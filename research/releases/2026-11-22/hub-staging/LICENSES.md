# Source-specific reuse and attribution

This Hub publication currently contains metadata and source/verification notices. **No raw GEO archive, PRIDE proteomics file or controlled EGA file is mirrored here.** The planned full archival release remains separate and unfinished. This notice does not assign one new licence to third-party source artifacts.

## Published metadata

The Silver catalogue is a factual, allowlisted transformation of official NCBI E-utilities/GEO SOFT and PRIDE REST metadata. Cite the original repositories, accession identifiers and linked studies. Source API URLs, retrieval timestamps, byte counts and response SHA-256 values are recorded in `silver/catalogue/2026-11-22/requests.jsonl`. Full raw API response bodies, contacts, unrestricted clinical text and individual twin phenotypes are not republished.

PRIDE PXD003403 currently declares **EBI terms of use** through its API. Its factual metadata entry is included; a retrospective CC0 licence is not asserted. `metadata/dataset-rights.json` retains source-specific evidence and unresolved questions. Catalogue inclusion does not expand rights to an upstream file.

## Linked GEO archives and local receipts

GEO's stated public-data use/distribution policy supports reuse while preserving source-owner claims: [disclaimer](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/info/disclaimer.html) and [FAQ](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/info/faq.html), reviewed 22 November 2026. The local checks found no source-specific distribution prohibition in the inspected metadata/archive scope. This is not a public-domain declaration or an assertion of an exclusive licence.

- GSE97362: [GEO](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE97362); Butcher et al., [DOI 10.1016/j.ajhg.2017.04.004](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ajhg.2017.04.004).
- GSE116300: [GEO](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE116300); [DOI 10.1038/s41431-017-0023-0](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41431-017-0023-0).
- GSE218186: [GEO](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE218186); publication identifiers are retained in the catalogue and rights register.

The original archives include source-supplied Illumina platform/manifest files. Their names, headers and attribution were preserved in the local originals; they are not relicensed by this project. A separate journal-article licence does not automatically describe its GEO files. No article full text is included in this metadata publication.

Original project documentation and source manifests may be reused with attribution to Rare Omics Atlas and their cited sources. That permission does not broaden third-party dataset terms or imply clinical validation. Byte-level publication checks and local source checks are distinct from a later study's scientific and institutional admission.
