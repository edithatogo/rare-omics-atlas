# Reference data, hosting and reuse

**Decision review: 22 November 2026.** These are source, bounded public-source qualification and architecture decisions; no patient files, ontology snapshots or derived results were uploaded. Public access, source research reuse, source redistribution, institutional analysis admission and artifact-specific cloud hosting are separate questions. Use the [source/intake policy](source-access-ethics.md), [rights synthesis](dataset-rights.md) and [per-accession rights register](registries/dataset-rights.json) together; none substitutes for the separate [file-integrity receipt](qualification/initial-source-receipt.md) or the applicable institutional determination.

## Keep the medallion structure small

Bronze, Silver and Gold describe the state of a file. They do not grant new rights or require three services, databases or Hugging Face repositories.

| Layer | What belongs here | Initial storage/publication decision |
|---|---|---|
| Bronze | Unmodified source files, retrieval URL, accession/revision, checksum, source terms and consent/use notices | Authorised local integrity/header checks may proceed where source terms support download. Keep bytes outside Git; record the actual retrieval and check scope. A first public release can contain accession/URL/hash manifests. A raw-file mirror is optional and requires source distribution support plus an artifact-specific disclosure/hosting decision |
| Silver | QC-qualified matrices, participant–specimen–assay map, masks, harmonised feature and ontology tables | Patient-level beta/M values and rare phenotype/variant combinations remain human molecular information. Parquet conversion or pseudonyms do not make them anonymous. Publish only approved, appropriately minimised projections |
| Gold | Frozen contrasts, effect/uncertainty tables, benchmark receipts, aggregate findings and manuscript figures | Preferred first public output after disclosure and rights review. A tiny stratum or identifying combination can remain sensitive even in a results table; synthetic fixtures are a useful openly reproducible alternative |

**Hugging Face is technically suitable** for versioned Parquet tables and other dataset files. It is not automatically an approved custodian for every human-data source, and a private or gated repository is not a substitute for permission, consent scope or institutional cloud approval. Keep raw files in their original formats; use Parquet for metadata and results and a labelled matrix format where appropriate. Retain the [interchange contract](interoperability.md).

The [Hub Terms](https://huggingface.co/terms-of-service) require the uploader to have the rights to post the content and grant hosting/service rights; public repositories allow broad access and reuse subject to accompanying licences. The [content policy](https://huggingface.co/content-policy) also applies. A repository-level licence tag cannot relicense its third-party inputs. Preserve source notices, attribution and restrictions per object; use mixed-licence metadata when necessary. Do not redistribute restricted OMIM/KEGG/HMDB content by calling it a derived Silver table.

The [GEO disclaimer](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/info/disclaimer.html) states that NCBI places no restrictions on use or distribution of GEO data and preserves possible source-owner rights. Its [FAQ](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/info/faq.html) requires appropriate permission for fully public deposition. The bounded rights review found no contrary source notice for the proposed secondary research in the inspected GEO seed material. Record this positive finding; do not leave every source “permission unknown” or demand unpublished consent forms by default. Inspect actual file notices and proposed outputs without treating an unseen notice as a known restriction. The institution's pathway remains separate, without assuming every public-data project needs a new full HREC application.

### Per-artifact release decision

The [29-accession rights register](registries/dataset-rights.json) distinguishes research reuse, raw/Bronze distribution, Silver distribution, aggregate Gold candidates and institutional status. It includes parent/subseries records and does not count 29 independent cohorts. Public GEO raw and Silver data are **conditional distribution candidates**, rather than universally prohibited. Exact file notices, required attribution, disclosure implications and the destination must be recorded before an actual upload. PXD003403 retains a specific legacy-licence question; unverified cell-model leads retain source-file qualification tasks. The newly checked GSE128801/GSE120428 arrays and GSE301386 chromatin-reference listings are source-qualified leads, not completed file or scientific qualifications. Human sequencing associated with GSE120428 remains controlled EGA material and is excluded from this public-source path.

Keep the article licence, data licence/notice and repository policy in distinct fields. An article's NC/ND restriction is not automatically imposed on independent numerical analysis of separate GEO data. A permissive article licence likewise does not relicense third-party datasets. The explicit CC0 data waivers found in some papers apply within their stated scope; retained notices and exceptions still matter.

For each intended HF object, record its accession inputs, source URLs/revisions, exact file or derivation hash, applicable notices, intended public/private visibility, disclosure assessment and institutional hosting disposition where applicable. Original aggregate results, benchmark summaries, analysis specifications and the atlas's own factual provenance catalogue are the preferred first outputs. Model weights, embeddings and individual prediction scores are separate output classes; a Gold label does not settle their rights or disclosure characteristics. A licence tag should describe rights actually held, never broaden source permissions.

For the first release, prefer **one public atlas metadata/results dataset**, registered in the existing account catalogue, with logical paths for eligible artefacts. Keep differently restricted data in separately controlled storage only if permitted. Do not create one repository per disease or per medallion layer. Hugging Face visibility applies to the repository, not independently to a Bronze folder. Use pinned commit revisions, file hashes and a dataset card that states sample/assay counts, limits, exclusions, provenance and intended use. Check [current storage limits](https://huggingface.co/docs/hub/storage-limits) and [dataset-upload guidance](https://huggingface.co/docs/hub/datasets-adding) before a real release; no quota/cost promise is made.

## Add annotation sources when they answer a question

More annotations do not create more independent observations. For the initial study, sample identity, molecular diagnosis, age, tissue, sex, control selection and blood-cell composition are more valuable than importing every database.

| Resource | When / role | Rights and scientific handling |
|---|---|---|
| **MONDO + OMIM identifiers** | Initial disease catalogue and explicit crosswalks; retain the original diagnosis and causal gene/variant mechanism | [MONDO](https://mondo.monarchinitiative.org/pages/download/) is CC BY 4.0. Preserve exact/narrow/broad mapping predicates and version. OMIM prose, bulk tables and API use have separate [terms](https://www.omim.org/help/agreement); identifiers are not permission to mirror all OMIM content |
| **HPO** | Initial disease-level annotations; participant-level phenotypes only when actually reported | Preserve positive/negative/unknown, evidence, onset/frequency and provenance. Disease annotations are not imputed patient observations. Follow the [HPO licence](https://human-phenotype-ontology.github.io/license.html), acknowledgement, version and source-integrity requirements |
| **HGNC / Ensembl / array manifests** | Initial stable gene/probe identity, genome build and CpG-to-region/gene mapping | [HGNC downloads](https://www.genenames.org/download/) and [Ensembl](https://www.ensembl.org/info/data/index.html) are identifier/reference resources. Freeze release/build, transcript policy and manifest/mask edition. Nearest gene is not automatically the regulated gene |
| **Reactome** | First pathway reference, after valid gene/region mapping | [Database data and derived data files are CC0](https://reactome.org/license); images/software have different terms. Freeze species/release and eligible gene universe. Enrichment is not a measurement of pathway activity |
| **Gene Ontology** | Initial complementary functional annotation; prespecify a limited analysis | [CC BY 4.0](https://www.geneontology.org/page/use-and-license). Keep ontology and annotation releases, evidence codes and tested universe. Overlapping GO/Reactome findings are correlated evidence, not independent replication |
| **UniProtKB** | Add for protein identifiers, function, complexes or proteomics; optional for first methylation-only study | [CC BY 4.0](https://www.uniprot.org/help/license). Preserve canonical/isoform and species mapping; gene, protein abundance and activity are different quantities |
| **ClinVar** | Add when variant-level cohort eligibility or mechanistic stratification needs it | [Public downloads](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/clinvar/docs/access/). Pin assertion date, submitter/review status and conflicts. A present-day annotation is not what was known when the original cohort was classified; do not silently relabel cases or infer phenotype |
| **Open Targets** | Later target-evidence context or perturbation prioritisation | [Platform data CC0 with source information](https://platform-docs.opentargets.org/licence). Retain evidence type/source and date. Disease–target associations used as predictors must not leak the held-out outcome or be counted again as independent validation |
| **ChEBI** | Add with metabolite/reaction data or a specified metabolic model | [CC BY 4.0](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/chebi/about/). Useful stable chemical identifiers; distinguish ionisation, stereochemistry and compartment. Does not supply concentrations or fluxes |
| **HMDB** | Later metabolomics interpretation and reference ranges, if genuinely required | [Published use terms](https://hmdb.ca/citing) limit commercial use/redistribution without permission. Check exact download terms; avoid bulk public mirroring by default. A reference range is not a measurement in these patients |
| **KEGG PATHWAY** | Optional replication of a published analysis or coverage gap after Reactome/GO | [Academic access and service/commercial terms](https://www.genome.jp/kegg/legal.html), [FTP subscription](https://www.kegg.jp/kegg/download/). API/site access does not imply unrestricted bulk redistribution. No mandatory KEGG dependency |
| **KEGG GENES** | Only if a KEGG-specific mapping/reaction consumer requires it | Same terms; HGNC/Ensembl/UniProt mappings usually cover initial needs without another identifier layer |
| **KEGG DISEASE** | Later curated disease-mechanism context if it adds information beyond MONDO/HPO | Same terms; curated knowledge is not independent omics and cannot replace actual cohort phenotyping |
| **Monarch Initiative** | Later cross-species disease/gene/phenotype evidence and semantic similarity | The [knowledge graph](https://monarchinitiative.org/kg/terms) aggregates sources with their own licences; its application's BSD licence is not a universal data licence. Retain species, edge predicate, evidence and primary source; avoid double-counting HPO/MONDO already imported |
| **NCBI MedGen** | Targeted identifier reconciliation, obsolete-ID history and ClinVar-linked condition mappings | [FTP report documentation](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/medgen/docs/ftp_info/) identifies source-specific mappings. It is an aggregator, including external/UMLS-derived material; do not assume all source prose is newly public domain |
| **EMBL-EBI OxO / OxO2** | Optional mapping aid for unresolved crosswalks | Prefer explicit, versioned SSSOM mappings. [OxO2's primary paper](https://ceur-ws.org/Vol-4111/Demo_Paper_1.pdf) explains why unrestricted xref chaining can produce unsound equivalence and adds predicates/provenance. Verify the actual service/API before integration; do not make the old OxO endpoint a required live dependency |

### Mapping and model safeguards

Keep a small provenance table: subject ID, mapping predicate, object ID, mapping set/version, evidence/source and confidence/disposition. Distinguish exact equivalence from relatedness or broader/narrower concepts. Do not merge conditions through arbitrary transitive xrefs, especially different allelic mechanisms in the same gene. Keep LoF/GoF, tissue and age strata where scientifically necessary.

Use immutable reference releases for one frozen analysis; refresh at a new N0/N1 with a documented mapping diff. Freeze knowledge used to construct predictions, hold out relevant outcome-derived edges, and compare molecular-only, ontology-only and combined baselines. A phenotype–methylation concordance test must not define both sides from the same disease label. Every additional source needs a consumer and an ablation or interpretive purpose; a universal biomedical knowledge graph is unnecessary initially.

## URL and evidence status

Primary landing pages/terms and the named public account cards were checked during this review. Some dynamic endpoints are not fully readable in this environment: OMIM's agreement was robots-blocked; the newer HPO app licence endpoint returned 404; HMDB's main citation page failed while its publisher-controlled test mirror supplied the use statement. These limitations do not become licence clearance. Preserve the earlier public HPO licence source and confirm exact current asset terms at intake.

The OxO landing page returned a JavaScript application shell. Third-party claims that all OxO endpoints are retired were not accepted as a verified provider-wide fact. OxO2's primary publication establishes the newer design; service deployment/API compatibility remains untested. No OxO API compatibility requests, assay downloads or checksum verification were performed merely by opening a URL. HGNC's current download documentation points to its Google Cloud Storage bucket; the Ensembl data page redirected to its June 2026 archive. Select an explicit release rather than assuming a landing URL always provides the latest edition.
