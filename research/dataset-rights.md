# Dataset reuse, redistribution and Hugging Face assessment

Reviewed **22 November 2026**. This is a bounded primary-source assessment of the atlas seeds and named technical/cell-model leads. It covers **29 accession records**, including SuperSeries and subseries that are **not additional independent cohorts**. The machine-readable evidence is in [registries/dataset-rights.json](registries/dataset-rights.json); the governing intake and hosting decisions are in [source-access-ethics.md](source-access-ethics.md) and [resources-hosting-reuse.md](resources-hosting-reuse.md). This review requested no original consent forms, made no institutional ethics determination and performed no public upload. Actual file retrieval and administrative checks are recorded in the [initial source receipt](qualification/initial-source-receipt.md) and [structured receipt](qualification/initial-source-receipt.json); this source-rights document does not overwrite those observed results.

## Decision

The publicly released GEO datasets have a **positive basis for access, research reuse and distribution**. The source review did not identify a notice forbidding the proposed secondary methylation/expression research in the reviewed material. They should not all remain labelled “permission unknown” merely because an individual Creative Commons licence or private consent form was not retrieved.

This does **not** make every proposed Hugging Face artifact automatically cleared. Record the exact source material, retained notices, disclosure risk and institutional hosting/research decision separately. A missing fact remains unknown, rather than evidence of a prohibition. The most useful immediate public artifact is the atlas's own accession/provenance/rights catalogue. The most useful later public artifacts are documented aggregate effects, QC summaries, analysis specifications and software benchmarks. Participant-level matrices can also be candidates; their release decision needs to be made for the actual artifact.

For the initial **GSE97362, GSE116300 and GSE218186** intake, no contrary notice was found that would forbid downloading public IDATs for bounded integrity/header checks. That administrative verification need not be blocked by an unissued institutional determination about the later scientific analysis or public cloud hosting.

## Repository policy and article licences are different evidence

| Source | Evidence checked | Consequence |
|---|---|---|
| [GEO disclaimer](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/info/disclaimer.html) | Short source excerpt: “NCBI places no restrictions on the use or distribution of the data contained in the GEO database.” The same page preserves source-owner IP claims. | Positive use/distribution evidence. GEO cannot waive an actual third-party restriction. |
| [GEO FAQ](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/info/faq.html) | Public records may be accessed, downloaded and reused. Submitters, including non-NIH submitters, must have appropriate permission for fully public release. | Public deposition is meaningful consent/release evidence; do not routinely demand unpublished consent forms or new author permission. |
| [GEO citation guidance](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/info/linking.html) | Original publication, repository and accession should be cited. | Preserve accession-level attribution and provenance in every derivative. |
| [Hugging Face terms](https://huggingface.co/terms-of-service), “Your Content” | Uploader must possess the necessary rights; service and public-repository licences permit hosting and further use. Existing customary licences are retained. | Choose a truthful dataset-card licence and preserve notices. A public upload grants broad downstream use through the service; private visibility does not create source permission. |
| [EMBL-EBI terms](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/about/terms-of-use/), revised 14 October 2026 | EBI adds no restrictions beyond original owners' terms unless resource-specific terms apply. | Positive repository policy, but do not infer an exact legacy PRIDE licence. |
| [PRIDE resources in 2022](https://academic.oup.com/nar/article/50/D1/D543/6415112) | PRIDE describes the move toward CC0 for prospective submissions from 2020; earlier submissions lacked a uniform licence. | **PXD003403, submitted in 2016, cannot be labelled CC0 from the later policy alone.** |

An article's **CC BY**, **CC BY-NC** or **CC BY-NC-ND** notice governs material within its scope. It does not by itself settle the separate GEO archive's licence. For example, the CC BY-NC-ND article associated with GSE97362 does not itself prohibit independent analysis of its publicly deposited numerical arrays. Conversely, a CC BY article does not automatically make every reused external dataset CC BY.

Three reviewed papers—GSE125367, GSE86833 and GSE92580—explicitly state that a CC0 waiver covers data made available in the article unless otherwise indicated. This is favourable additional evidence for those materials. Preserve exceptions and do not extend the waiver to unrelated third-party inputs.

## Per-accession evidence and disposition

**GEO-supported** below means publicly released source data have affirmative repository reuse/distribution support and no contrary notice was found in the material reviewed. It is a source-level finding, not a claim that every archive was opened or that institutional research review is complete.

**Conditional HF** means raw/Bronze and participant-level Silver are distribution candidates after exact-file notices and actual disclosure/hosting decisions are recorded. **Aggregate candidate** means original Gold results may be released after an artifact-specific assessment; aggregation alone does not confer a licence or guarantee non-identifiability.

## What to store in each medallion layer

| Artifact | Recommended default | Required recorded evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Accession/source catalogue, own factual summaries and derived schema | Public Git/HF candidate now; no participant matrices needed | Sources, review date, evidence limitations, honest licence for original catalogue. Link to article text rather than copying full abstracts or papers. |
| Bronze original archives | Keep a local/institutionally approved cache first; HF mirror is optional, not required for reproducibility | Exact source URL, file hash/size, original notices, source-policy basis, actual hosting/disclosure decision. Keep source archive intact. |
| Silver cleaned matrices | Arrow/Parquet may improve interchange, but do not change rights or make rare-disease data anonymous | Derivation lineage, retained notices, participant/assay distinction, minimal metadata and specific release decision. |
| Gold DMP/DMR effects, pathway/module results and QC summaries | Strongest public-output candidates | Show what was aggregated, which sources contributed and whether a result or model exposes participant-linked information. Preserve relevant input notices. |
| Model weights, embeddings, individual prediction scores | Assess as their own output class | Do not assume “model” or “Gold” implies safe release. Avoid public individual scores unless explicitly intended and justified. |
| Withheld/controlled/request-only data | Exclude from the no-application seed path | Source access conditions; a private HF repository is not an exemption from those conditions. |

A project-level source manifest can describe all layers while the data remain in their original repositories or approved storage. A medallion design does **not** require copying every layer to Hugging Face. Prefer a reproducible manifest and accession-level downloads over an unnecessary public mirror.

## Completing source intake without inventing clearance

The initial track should store independent status fields for:

1. **Source public release**: accession verified and no application required for listed material.
2. **File integrity verification**: exact file fetched, hash and format checked; applies only to the tested file.
3. **Source reuse/distribution assessment**: policy plus source-specific notices and published consent/release evidence, including unresolved scope.
4. **Institutional research pathway**: documented exemption/review/governance decision, if the responsible institution requires it for the actual protocol.
5. **Scientific eligibility**: diagnosis, tissue, controls, assay independence and QC.
6. **Artifact release**: explicit decision for each intended public file or output.

This audit completes the bounded source-level assessment; it cannot issue the institution's determination or certify every future artifact. Carry the remaining facts as named follow-up tasks, rather than making the track depend on hypothetical author permission or private consent forms that have not been shown to be necessary. Recheck notices at download and publication/release time, and record changed source terms alongside the literature/novelty refresh.

## Evidence limits

The assessment used official GEO indexes/pages, PubMed/PMC metadata and rights APIs, rights-eligible PMC article bodies, publisher pages, EMBL-EBI/PRIDE policy and Hugging Face terms. Some PMC bodies omit ethics/data-availability back matter; PMC retrieval availability is not the same as publisher open access. Bounded article text or an inaccessible publisher page is explicitly marked where it prevented a finding.

No absence-of-restriction finding is presented as a warranty that an unseen file contains no notice. Conversely, an unseen notice is not treated as an actual restriction. Dataset provenance and file checks are more informative next steps than repeating a generic “all permissions unknown” status.

The [companion JSON register](registries/dataset-rights.json) contains one row per accession and distinct article-licence, source-use, raw/Silver/Gold, institutional-status and unresolved-fact fields. This integration applies Ponytail's existing-file-first approach: reuse the repository's intake/hosting policies, retain one source register and add no data service, custom rights engine or new approval dependency.
