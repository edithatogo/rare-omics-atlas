## Six separate questions

1. **Repository access:** Is the source publicly released and linked for download without an account, access committee or application?
2. **Download verification:** Has the specific file actually been fetched and its hash/format checked? Record the file, bytes checked, result and limitations in the intake receipt. Keep untested files unverified.
3. **Source research reuse:** What affirmative repository policy, source-specific terms and published consent/release evidence support the intended analysis? Public GEO release provides positive support. No contrary notice was identified for the proposed secondary research within the reviewed scope; uninspected file notices remain unknown.
4. **Source redistribution:** Do the applicable terms support distributing the exact original or derivative? GEO itself imposes no use/distribution restriction, but source-owner claims and any actual file-specific conditions remain relevant. An article licence is a separate field, not an inferred licence for every repository input.
5. **Institutional research admission:** What exemption/review/governance pathway applies to the actual protocol at the responsible institution? No determination has been issued here. Public GEO status is not an institutional exemption, and it does not automatically imply that a full new HREC application is required.
6. **Artifact and hosting admission:** May the specific raw file, Silver matrix, aggregate result or model be released to Hugging Face under its source terms, disclosure assessment and applicable institutional hosting conditions? Decide this per artifact. Private visibility, Parquet conversion and aggregation do not create rights.

NCBI states that anybody can access public GEO without login and that files can be downloaded and reused. Its FAQ requires submitters, including non-NIH submitters, to have appropriate permission for fully public release. NCBI imposes no restriction itself on GEO data use/distribution **but cannot waive source submitters' intellectual-property claims**. Sources: https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/info/faq.html ; https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/info/download.html ; https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/info/disclaimer.html . These are affirmative source-use/release evidence, not a review of each original participant consent form. Do not require unpublished consent forms or new author permission by default for publicly deposited data.

For Hugging Face hosting, source-specific ontology licences and existing account assets, use [resources, hosting and reuse](resources-hosting-reuse.md). The [rights assessment](dataset-rights.md) separates source research use, distribution and raw/Silver/Gold hosting dispositions. The initial GSE97362/GSE116300/GSE218186 source review found no notice prohibiting bounded local IDAT integrity/header checks. Those authorised administrative checks do not confer scientific-analysis or public-upload approval. No dataset has been uploaded or mirrored by this review.

### What the article licence does and does not establish

GSE97362's [source article](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28475860/) is CC BY-NC-ND 4.0; the Sotos GSE191274/GSE191276 [article](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35094088/) is CC BY-NC 4.0. These notices do not automatically restrict independent analysis of separate publicly deposited GEO numerical files, nor do CC BY articles automatically license every external input. GSE125367 and the two technical benchmark articles have explicit data-waiver language with stated scope/exceptions. Retain article and dataset rights separately, including for copied figures or supplements. PXD003403 is a 2016 legacy PRIDE record: do not assign it CC0 solely from the prospective post-2020 policy described by [PRIDE](https://academic.oup.com/nar/article/50/D1/D543/6415112).

## Human research ethics and reuse in Australia

The [NHMRC National Statement (2025)](https://www.nhmrc.gov.au/research-policy/ethics/national-statement-ethical-conduct-human-research) is effective from **23 June 2026**, confirmed on the official page during this review. Section 3.1 addresses secondary use of information, scope of consent, reasonable participant expectations, privacy and identifiability. Exemption is an **institutional or appropriate review-body determination**, not a declaration the dataset downloader can make. This is particularly important for small, rare-disease molecular cohorts with variant/phenotype metadata and reidentification risk. NHMRC's current page describes review of selected provisions on the definition of human research and exemption criteria; check the final operative guidance and [consultation notices](https://consultations.nhmrc.gov.au/) before the research protocol is lodged.

Queensland Health [research ethics and governance HSD QH-HSD-035](https://www.health.qld.gov.au/system-governance/policies-standards/health-service-directives/research%2C-ethics-and-governance) applies when an HHS collaborates or research is undertaken at an HHS. [Metro North's guidance](https://metronorth.health.qld.gov.au/research/ethics/exempt) includes public-domain data as an example potentially eligible for exemption and describes **submitting for formal exemption determination**. A CHHHS/HHS-affiliated project should document its institutional review pathway and governance even when source data are entirely public. Do **not** infer that reuse of existing GEO matrices requires a fresh HREC application in every circumstance either: request the appropriate exemption/low-risk/HREC determination based on the actual protocol and material.

If future work uses Queensland Health clinical records, consented local biospecimens, identifiable data, linked case information or controlled-access datasets, reassess research ethics and site authorisation separately. Existing public-data status does not carry over to new clinical source material. Avoid public Git commits of patient-level matrices, combinations of rare variant + age + phenotype, or participant-linked redistributable datasets until permission and privacy decisions are documented.

### Suggested institutional determination packet (not a submitted application)

A protocol limited to public open-access, previously deposited data; source accession and sponsor/publication; anticipated reuse purpose; exactly which files and metadata are needed; whether personal information is present or reasonably re-identifiable; consent/use-notice audit; download/storage/retention plan; public output disclosure limits; source terms; intended collaborator institutions; and a request for **written determination** of ethics review requirements and any relevant HHS governance pathway.

## Ontology and software licensing are separate

- **MONDO:** open CC BY 4.0; cite and pin release: https://mondo.monarchinitiative.org/pages/download/ .
- **HPO:** freely usable, subject to the HPO project's actual licence requirements for acknowledgement, version and integrity of source ontology files: https://human-phenotype-ontology.github.io/license.html . Do not assume HPO itself is CC0 or that modifications may be redistributed as an official HPO release.
- **OMIM:** research usage is encouraged; identifiers and certain tables are accessible openly, but bulk gene–phenotype downloads may require registration and public redistribution/integration may require licensing. Avoid copying OMIM proprietary prose into the repository: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6323937/ .
- **GEO** and **ProteomeXchange:** public file availability is not a blanket copyright, consent, privacy or commercial use determination; record conditions at each accession.

## Independent admission records for T01

A dataset can be **discovered_public** on repository evidence and **source_reuse_supported** / **source_redistribution_supported** on the documented source policies and notices. These findings need not await a private consent form or an institutional determination. A finding that no contrary notice was identified is bounded by the inspected sources and files; it is not a universal warranty.

Record **download_verified** only for an actually retrieved file with hash and format checks, including the scope of any header-only check. Record **research_reuse_eligible** after the intended protocol's source conditions and applicable institutional ethics/governance pathway are documented. Record **model_eligible** only after diagnosis, controls, participant/specimen independence and QC are established. Record **artifact_release_eligible** separately for each proposed public object and destination, preserving notices and any qualifications.

The bounded source review and administrative integrity checks can be completed while institutional analysis or hosting admission remains pending. A missing fact is *unknown*, not approved and not automatically prohibited. Carry concrete unresolved facts forward with their affected action; do not make every stage wait for a blanket “all rights cleared” label.
